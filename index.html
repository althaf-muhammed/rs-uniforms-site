What we check.

Four things we look at before any order leaves us.

  • Fabric

    Chosen for the work it has to do. Breathable for schools and hospitals, hard wearing for factories, smart for offices.

  • Stitching

    Strong seams where a garment takes the most strain, so it lasts through daily wear and washing.

  • Fit

    A full range of sizes, so every person on the team looks right and feels comfortable.

  • Finish

    Clean collars, straight hems, and logos placed the same way on every piece.

Branding

  1. i.

    Embroidery

    Neat and long lasting. Best for logos on shirts, polos, blazers and coats.

  2. ii.

    Screen printing

    Good value for large runs of t-shirts and event wear.

  3. iii.

    DTF and heat transfer

    Full colour logos and small batches, with sharp detail.

  4. iv.

    Sublimation

    All over prints for sports jerseys. The colour goes into the fabric, so it does not crack or peel.
