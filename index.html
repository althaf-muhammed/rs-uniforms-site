05 · Branding and fabric

Your logo, your fabric.

Every uniform can carry your name, done the way that suits the garment.

  1. i.

    Embroidery

    Neat and long lasting. Best for logos on shirts, polos, blazers and coats.

  2. ii.

    Screen printing

    Good value for large runs of t-shirts and event wear.

  3. iii.

    DTF and heat transfer

    Full colour logos and small batches, with sharp detail.

  4. iv.

    Sublimation

    All over prints for sports jerseys. The colour goes into the fabric, so it does not crack or peel.

Fabric

Cotton, poly cotton, polyester, drill and knit fabrics, in the colours you need. We show you swatches before anything is cut.

Send us your logo file or a photo of it. We will suggest the best method for each garment.
